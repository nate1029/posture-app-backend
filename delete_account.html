Account Deletion Request

We're sorry to see you go! If you wish to permanently delete your NudgeUp account and all associated data, you can do so directly within the app.

What happens when you delete your account?

  • Your profile and login credentials are permanently removed.
  • All your posture history, session logs, and analytics are erased from our servers.
  • Your gamification progress, streaks, and points are reset.
  • Any anonymous crash reports linked to your account ID are purged.
  • Local data stored on your device is cleared.

This action cannot be undone.

How to delete your account:

  1. Open the NudgeUp app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings tab (the gear icon on the home screen).
  3. Scroll down to the bottom to find the Account section.
  4. Tap on the Delete Account button.
  5. Confirm by tapping Delete Everything in the dialog.

If you no longer have access to the app or your device, you can request manual account deletion by emailing us from the address associated with your account at privacy@nudgeup.app. We will process your request within 7 business days.
